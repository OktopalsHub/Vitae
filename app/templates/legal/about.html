About

Job matching built for people who apply seriously

Vitae helps you spend less time guessing and more time on roles that actually fit your CV.

What we do

You upload a CV once. Vitae scores open listings against your skills and experience, explains the match, and generates an apply pack — tailored resume, cover note, and form answers — when you are ready to apply.

Why we built it

Job boards show everything. Application tools often genericize your story. Vitae sits in the middle: personal scoring first, then targeted materials per role, so you can apply with confidence instead of spam.

How we work

  • Your data stays yours. Profiles and drafts belong to your account. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Transparent pricing. Free tier to start; monthly plans unlock the full board and AI. Cancel anytime — details in Terms.
  • Honest AI. Generated copy is a draft based on your profile. You review everything before you submit.
